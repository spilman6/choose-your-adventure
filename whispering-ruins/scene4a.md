# Scene 4A 

You step through the wind archway and find yourself on a narrow stone bridge suspended over an endless abyss. Gales howl around you, threatening to sweep you off your feet. Across the bridge lies a gleaming chamber. 

If you drop to your hands and knees to crawl across safely, go to [Scene 5a](./scene5a.md).

If you try to run across the bridge before the wind picks up strength, go to [Scene 5b](./scene5b.md).
