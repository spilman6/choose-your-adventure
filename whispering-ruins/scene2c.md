# Scene 2C

The double doors creak loudly, echoing through a grand, pillared hall. At the far end, a massive stone guardian statue awakens, its glowing red eyes locking onto you. It raises a colossal stone sword and blocks the only exit. However, there is a small crawl space on the wall next to you that seems to be oozing out purple goo.
