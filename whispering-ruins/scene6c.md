# Scene 6C

You notice a loose floor tile beneath the pedestal. Lifting it reveals a hidden brass lever. Pulling it triggers a secret sliding panel in the wall that smells faintly of sulfur and dried herbs. 

If you step through the hidden panel into an abandoned alchemical laboratory filled with bubbling green potions, go to [Scene 7a](./scene7a.md).

If you ignore the lab and trigger the lever a second time to reseal it, returning to the main mirror room, go to [Scene 6b](./scene6b.md).
