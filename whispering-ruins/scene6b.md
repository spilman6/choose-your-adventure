# Scene 6B

The oak door swings open to reveal a magnificent subterranean throne room. Piles of ancient gold and jewels glitter in the torchlight, and at the head of the room sits a velvet-lined dais. 

If you immediately begin filling your pockets with treasure, go to [Scene 7a](./scene7a.md).

If you ignore the gold and approach the dais directly, go to [Scene 7b](./scene7b.md).
