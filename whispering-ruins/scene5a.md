# Scene 5A

You push through a velvet curtain and enter a hexagonal room lined with floor-to-ceiling mirrors. Your reflections move independently, smiling mockingly. In the center of the room sits a pedestal holding a golden key. 

If you smash the center mirror to find the hidden passage behind it, go to [Scene 6a](./scene6a.md).

If you take the golden key from the pedestal and unlock the heavy oak door ahead, go to [Scene 6b](./scene6b.md).

If you check beneath the pedestal for a secret lever, go to [Scene 6c](./scene6c.md).
