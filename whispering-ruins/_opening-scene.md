
# The Whispering Ruins

### Introduction

You stand at the edge of the Whispering Ruins, an ancient, vine-choked labyrinth rumored to hold the legendary Chrono-Crystal. Local folklore claims that whoever possesses the crystal can command time itself, but those who enter unprepared become lost in its shifting corridors forever. 

Clutching a weathered map and a flickering torch, you push open the heavy, rusted iron gates. The air grows cold, and the heavy scent of damp stone fills your nostrils. Ahead, the path splits into three distinct tunnels. You must choose your route carefully.
