# Sun-mist Canyon #

<sup>By <a href="https://github.com/Atarie3">Ashton Hawkinson</a> and <a href="https://github.com/joshua-nett">Joshua Nett</a></sup>

You end up never taking a contract, after a time you lay down your sword and pick up a lute. You took up being a bard and sing the praises of heroes you wished to become but never had the courage to follow. You wander the kingdoms spreading stories and becoming famous in your own right, but it never feels like enough.
