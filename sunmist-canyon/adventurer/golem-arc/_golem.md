# Sun-mist Canyon #

<sup>By <a href="https://github.com/Atarie3">Ashton Hawkinson</a> and <a href="https://github.com/joshua-nett">Joshua Nett</a></sup>

You head to the mountain pass and see ice on the road, it is the end of the summer months, snow and ice should not yet be around. You draw your bow and get ready, the golems are near. The battle is fierce, a bow is not great against a creature of magic, alas you are able to push the golems off the cliff face of the passes. Through the battle, frostbite took your hands. They will never work the same again.
