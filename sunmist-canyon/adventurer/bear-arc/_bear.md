# Sun-mist Canyon #

<sup>By <a href="https://github.com/Atarie3">Ashton Hawkinson</a> and <a href="https://github.com/joshua-nett">Joshua Nett</a></sup>

You head to the bear cave and lay in wait for it to return. A little jerky at the mouth of the cave should lure it! The bear shows up an hour later looking hungry, it sees the jerky you laid out early and greedily eats it all in one bite.

Looking closer you see that the bear looks starved, it appears to not have eaten for weeks. Could make it easier to take down, or is there another way?
