# Sun-mist Canyon #

<sup>By <a href="https://github.com/Atarie3">Ashton Hawkinson</a> and <a href="https://github.com/joshua-nett">Joshua Nett</a></sup>

You pull out another few pieces of jerky and toss them around the clearing to distract the bear for a little bit. Once it catches the scent of the jerky, it gets up from its post at the mouth of the cave and hobbles over to the closest piece of jerky. As soon as it reaches the first piece of jerky, you bolt for the entrance of the cave and duck inside before the bear can turn back around.

It is dark at the mouth of the cave, far too dark for only being a few feet in, and yet it is nearly pitch black as you dash inside. You stumble in the darkness as you slow down to a walk. After a few more steps the darkness seems to lift and you can see around you again, yet the mouth of the cave is still darker than night. This is all very strange, light should not work like that and bears shouldn’t act like a guard dog! No matter, you continue to trace a path deeper into the cave until you start seeing odd symbols on the walls. They give you chills just looking at them, and they get far more abundant on the walls the further you walk. You come to a final bend in the cave and round the corner to see a room; the walls are covered in those strange symbols and seem to even overlap. There are eerie tools laying on wooden tables around the room, and standing in the center of the room is a hunched over man. He looks decrepit, his black robes are torn and stained, his skin looks raw and cracked in places, and his eyes... His eyes are purely black, lacking any white sclera or iris at all. The man, now very apparently a dark mage opens his mouth and howls “TRESPASSSSEEERRRR!!!!!” it hurts your ears to listen. “YOU WILL PERRISH IN THE NAME OF THE DARK LORD!” His shrill voice and the echo make you want to cover your ears, but that would be dumb. The dark mage starts charging a dark magic into his hand; this is no time to stand idle. You drop into a low stance, lift your sword, and lunge. Your sword strikes true before the dark mage can cast his foul magic, and he falls. You have won, and you have rid this world of a despicable person that used their gifts for evil.

In the end, you were celebrated as a hero within your town and went on to adventure far and wide. Even the dragons learned of your name.  Riches and good fortune followed in your footsteps for years to come before you retired to a castle in the mountains overlooking the town you grew up in.
