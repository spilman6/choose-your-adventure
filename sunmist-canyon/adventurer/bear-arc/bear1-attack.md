# Sun-mist Canyon #

<sup>By <a href="https://github.com/Atarie3">Ashton Hawkinson</a> and <a href="https://github.com/joshua-nett">Joshua Nett</a></sup>

You draw your sword and charge, you slash at the bear and it swipes at you. You catch its shoulder and your sword bounces off, it catches you in the legs and you go flying into the trees. Luckily the bear decided you weren’t appetizing and did not follow you for a snack, however your legs are broken. You will have to drag yourself back to town and hope you can see the doctor right away. Worse than that though, your time adventuring is surely done for now.
