# Sun-mist Canyon #

<sup>By <a href="https://github.com/Atarie3">Ashton Hawkinson</a> and <a href="https://github.com/joshua-nett">Joshua Nett</a></sup>

You move closer to the sound of singing, the voice sounds startlingly familiar. You accidentally splash some water moving to fast and the singing cuts off. Not seeing or hearing anything further, you call out " Is anyone there?" And immediately someone pops out from behind the plants on the riverbank. A mirror copy of... _yourself_? Startled, you both stare at each other for a while, it feels like an eternity. Overcoming the shock of it, you move closer and inspect this other you, they are a mirror copy except they're not wearing armor, and they are cleaner than you are.

This isn't right; they must be a doppelgänger. It must be _something_ but it cannot be you.
