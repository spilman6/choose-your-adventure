# Sun-mist Canyon #

<sup>By <a href="https://github.com/Atarie3">Ashton Hawkinson</a> and <a href="https://github.com/joshua-nett">Joshua Nett</a></sup>

You raise your sword and charge out of the water; the thing trips over itself in a scramble to run away. Tripping was the last mistake it would ever make. Turns out, it was also the last mistake you would ever make as well. That thing was not a doppelgänger; it was you. It was a fragment of the possibility that you could have become; and you destroyed it.

Its memories flood your head, driving you mad with the realization and grief of what you have done. You hold the fragment of your potential and the tears flow. You will never leave this spot; you do not deserve to move on after what you did.
