# Sun-mist Canyon #

<sup>By <a href="https://github.com/Atarie3">Ashton Hawkinson</a> and <a href="https://github.com/joshua-nett">Joshua Nett</a></sup>

You hesitate for only a moment longer, you cannot allow for this thing to steal your face, who you are. You reach for a flashbolt potion hanging from your belt, the thing charges at you, and you throw the potion. It strikes true, and the thing goes down with a crash. It will not be getting back up.  

As the adrenaline fades, you take a moment to register what you have done. That was no doppelganger; that was YOU.  How could this be? There is only one of you, so how could you now be lying in front of yourself? The world begins to spin around you as you realize what you have done. It couldn’t be helped, could it? The copy charged at you with a sword in hand! It was only defense... but the excuse sounds hollow to you as you slump over and the world goes black.
