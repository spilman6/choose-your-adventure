# Sun-mist Canyon #

<sup>By <a href="https://github.com/Atarie3">Ashton Hawkinson</a> and <a href="https://github.com/joshua-nett">Joshua Nett</a></sup>

You enter the cave, after stepping inside past the curtain of darkness, it all seems to lift at once. You can't see back out of the cave, but you can see ahead of you, and the cave is a lot deeper than you thought. Traveling further in, you come to a bend in the cave. Rounding the corner, you stop in your tracks as there are glyphs, EVERYWHERE. It seems like there isn't a bare patch of stone anywhere, and standing in the center of the room is the most decrepit human you have ever seen. Wearing torn and stained black robes, the man himself has stained and wrinkled skin, his sunken eyes lacking any white sclera, just pitch-black orbs that seem to devour light the longer you stare.  He knew you were coming. “YOU DARE TRESPASS HERE” his voice booms through the cave, much louder than you would expect from someone in his sorry state. “YOU SHALL PERISH IN THE NAME OF THE DARK LORD” the dark mage is irate, dark magic coalescing in his hands to strike you down. Too bad for him, you can throw faster than he can chant. The potion leaves your hand and streaks across the room. The dark mage goes to dodge but can't move when chanting. He instead catches it with his face and becomes a non-threat like the bear.

This dark mage was planning to use your town as a sacrifice to summon his dark lord; you stopped him with a bottle of stuff you happened to mix together.  Not all heroes wear capes or wield a sword it would seem.
