# Sun-mist Canyon #

<sup>By <a href="https://github.com/Atarie3">Ashton Hawkinson</a> and <a href="https://github.com/joshua-nett">Joshua Nett</a></sup>

You keep low along the reeds on the riverbank trying to hide from whatever is moving through the water. As you draw nearer, you hear a voice call out. "Is anyone there?" The voice sounds startlingly familiar. Way too familiar to be a mistake. Is that you?  

You pop up from the reeds you were hiding behind and spot... yourself? Crouched in the water is a perfect copy of yourself, appearing to have been washing themselves and their armor while still wearing it. Not sure of what to do, you both just stare for a while in shock. After an eternity both of you come forward and inspect. It is definitely you, only bruised and dirtier from what looks like mud.  

This isn't right; they must be a doppelgänger. It must be something but it cannot be you.
