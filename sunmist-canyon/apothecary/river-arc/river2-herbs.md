# Sun-mist Canyon #

<sup>By <a href="https://github.com/Atarie3">Ashton Hawkinson</a> and <a href="https://github.com/joshua-nett">Joshua Nett</a></sup>

You quickly speed up gathering herbs. Now staying quiet so whatever is in the water does not see you. Once all the herbs are gathered, you make a mad dash back towards that Alchemists hut and set to work brewing your potions and elixirs to practice your craft. Among your favorite to make is a thrown potion that explodes into fun colors when thrown. It packs a decent punch too, proved by the new doorway in the Alchemists house.

Rumors at the Adventurers guild are that there is a bear south of the town that has been taking livestock from the local farmers. Maybe you could test your new toys and claim the bears  bounty.
