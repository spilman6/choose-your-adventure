# Sun-mist Canyon #

<sup>By <a href="https://github.com/Atarie3">Ashton Hawkinson</a> and <a href="https://github.com/joshua-nett">Joshua Nett</a></sup>

You head back to the alchemist's hut, satisfied with your accomplishments for the day. Nothing else happens after that, and it is okay with you. Adventure and conquest were never your thing; you just wanted to mix things together and maybe help some fellow people out.

You continue learning under the towns Alchemist for years. You have a real talent for making potions! So much so that you get a recommendation to a school of alchemy in the big city! You spend many more years at the prestigious big city academy and become a famous apothecary and alchemist; known for all the useful inventions you have made over the years.
