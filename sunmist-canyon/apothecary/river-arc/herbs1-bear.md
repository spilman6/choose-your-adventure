# Sun-mist Canyon #

<sup>By <a href="https://github.com/Atarie3">Ashton Hawkinson</a> and <a href="https://github.com/joshua-nett">Joshua Nett</a></sup>

You head to the bear cave with your new toys at your belt and confidence only seen in legends. This bear won’t know what hit it, not that it's intelligent enough to know what alchemy is.  

Upon finding the bear cave, you spot said bear standing just outside, appearing to be watching for something? Odd, but no matter! You take aim with your potion bottle and THROW with all your might! It hits the bear in the side and does... very little other than upset it. You pause, register that the bear is looking at you, and grab another potion. Another one will surely do the trick! The bear charges, you duck behind a tree at the last second, and the bear smacks into the tree with force enough to shake the earth itself. Stunned, the bear sits up with its mouth open in bewilderment, this is the perfect chance, you take aim with the second potion and throw it straight into the bears mouth. The bear, now becoming a permanent non-threat, retains a permanent surprised look on its face.

Bear taken care of, you peek inside the cave. It seems oddly dark; the light seems to fade only a few feet inside the entrance.
