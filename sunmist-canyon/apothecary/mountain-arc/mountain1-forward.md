You continue up the path and step on a patch of ice, your foot does not rise for the next step. Looking down, it has been encased entirely in ice. Luckily you have a potion for that! You dump a liquid fire potion on the ice and watch it melt when you get smacked from behind by something cold. Frost golems have surrounded you and your potion belt is woefully unequipped.
