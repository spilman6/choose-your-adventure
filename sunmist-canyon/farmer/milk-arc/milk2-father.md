# Sun-mist Canyon #

<sup>By <a href="https://github.com/Atarie3">Ashton Hawkinson</a> and <a href="https://github.com/joshua-nett">Joshua Nett</a></sup>

You tell your father about the cattle, and he gets a haunted look in his eyes. He tells you to grab a pitchfork and follow him. You travel for days without him explaining a word. He handed you a backpack before you left. It had food, water, gold, and clothes. Eventually you arrive at a port city and rent a ship to travel afar.  Years later, you hear a story concerning the town you once lived in. It was destroyed by a dark mage, and adventurers were barely able to take him down afterward.
