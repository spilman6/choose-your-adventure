# Sun-mist Canyon #

<sup>By <a href="https://github.com/Atarie3">Ashton Hawkinson</a> and <a href="https://github.com/joshua-nett">Joshua Nett</a></sup>

You left it to the adventurers and they did more than expected. The bear was not the culprit, bigger still the bear was only a pile of bones. A dark mage had been using the cave as his hideout and using the farm animals as sacrifices for a large spell. Thanks to you reporting your missing livestock to the Adventurers guild, they pushed the contract to high priority. It saved the lives of the whole town.
