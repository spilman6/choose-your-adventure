# Sun-mist Canyon #

<sup>By <a href="https://github.com/Atarie3">Ashton Hawkinson</a> and <a href="https://github.com/joshua-nett">Joshua Nett</a></sup>

You look around for signs of what happened to the cattle; there are not even footprints.

This is unprecedented, how can cows just disappear without leaving any traces of where they went?  You go and walk the fields in search of the cattle; perhaps they are out to pasture? Alas, they are not out to pasture; they are entirely gone.
