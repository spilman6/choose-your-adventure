# Sun-mist Canyon #

<sup>By <a href="https://github.com/Atarie3">Ashton Hawkinson</a> and <a href="https://github.com/joshua-nett">Joshua Nett</a></sup>

You arrive at the cave and see no signs of a bear. You walk up to the mouth of the cave and are unable to see past 3 feet inside. That is unusual; light doesn't work like that. You go into the darkness holding a pitchfork tightly; the darkness recedes after a while sloughing off of you like steam. Looking back towards the entrance of the cave, it is pitch black, yet the rest of the cave going deeper seems to be lit by nothing at all. You travel deep into the cave, eventually coming across eerie symbols painted everywhere. They are on the walls, floor, and even ceiling. The further you continue to walk the denser the symbols get, overlapping each other in different colors. Eventually you come to a bend in the cave, rounding the bend you see a decrepit man in black robes standing in the middle of the chamber ahead of you. His eyes are pitch black, lacking an iris and sclera; his skin is patchy and scarred. He screams at you when you come into view, “TRESSPASSSSSSSSEEERRRRRR!!!!!!!” His voice is deafening in the cave. Far louder than it should be, he booms again “BE SACRIFICED IN THE NAME OF THE DARK LORD!”. The man starts to gather an ominous energy into his hands; it glows and pulses with an impossible black light. You turn to run, but he finishes casting before you can turn the corner...
